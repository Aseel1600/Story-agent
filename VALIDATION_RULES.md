# RAXE VALIDATION RULES

A project passes only when all critical checks pass.

## STORY

- coherent beginning, middle, ending
- child-safe conflict
- emotional clarity
- age 5–8 appropriate
- no unresolved dangerous ending

## CHARACTER

- identity remains stable
- age remains stable
- facial identity remains stable
- hairstyle remains stable
- clothing identity remains stable
- accessories remain stable
- personality remains stable

## WORLD

- locations belong to the established world
- architecture remains coherent
- palette remains coherent
- recurring objects remain coherent

## IMAGE

- 4:5 composition
- characters readable
- narrative focal point clear
- no accidental text
- no unwanted logos
- no malformed anatomy
- no contradictory props
- no style imitation of protected creators

## ARCHETYPE SAFETY

If the story uses a Cinderella-inspired concept, do NOT introduce:

- glass slipper
- pumpkin carriage
- fairy godmother
- prince-centered rescue
- royal-ball rescue structure
- stepmother-centered conflict

The story should center on original internal growth, practical creativity, kindness, friendship, community, and self-worth.

## PRODUCTS

All derivative products must agree with Story Book Canon.

## PASS/FAIL

Critical failure = automatic repair.
